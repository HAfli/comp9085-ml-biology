# Week 9 lab: clusters, and whether to believe them

```
COMP9085_Week09_Lab.ipynb   the notebook (no answers): 9 core tasks + 3 optional extensions, about two hours
COMP9085_Week09_Lab.html    the same notebook, rendered for reading in a browser
data/                       the Week 7 proteins: 23 classical descriptors and ESM-2 embeddings (see data/README.md)
```

WDBC (569 tumours, 30 features) ships with scikit-learn, so it needs no file. Keep `data/` next to the notebook; in
Google Colab the notebook downloads the two protein files from the course website. Requirements: Python 3.10+, numpy,
pandas, matplotlib, scipy, scikit-learn ≥ 1.3. No GPU. The unfilled notebook runs top to bottom in about a minute.

The matching website exercises are on the Week 9 page:
https://hafli.github.io/comp9085-ml-biology/weeks/week09.html

Built by `data/scripts/23_build_week09_lab.py` from the numbers in `data/scripts/week09_numbers.json`
(`22_build_week09_data.py`). Instructor solutions are released on Canvas after the lab; they are not in this repository.
